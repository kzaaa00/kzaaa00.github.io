▼ How to play & tips

How to play

  1. You are in an elevator holding the door open button while a stranger walks toward the doors.
  2. Tap to let go of the button. The doors take one second to close.
  3. Let go at exactly the right time so the doors shut right behind them. That is a perfect score.

Tips

  • The doors need a full second, so you have to let go before the person is inside, not after.
  • Let go too early and the doors close on them. Too late and everyone stands there waiting for the doors.

About this game

Holding the elevator for someone is polite, but timing it so the doors close the moment they step in feels like a tiny victory. This game turns that everyday moment into a one-second timing challenge.
