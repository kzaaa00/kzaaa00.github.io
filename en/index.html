tapitta

Tiny games for your phone, a few seconds each. Squeezing ketchup, peeling labels, finding the end of the tape: the small everyday moments that are harder than they look. No sign-up, no download. Share your score on X with a result image.

  • Hold the elevator. Let go at exactly the right time.

    You’re holding the door open button for a stranger. The doors take 1 second to close. Let go so they shut right behind them.

  • Get exactly one pump of shampoo.

    Hold to push the pump down, let go to stop. Fill the quarter-sized circle exactly for 100%. A little more comes out after you let go.

  • Draw a ketchup heart without going over the lines.

    Trace a ketchup heart on an omelette in one stroke. Slow makes it thick, fast makes it thin. Cover the dotted line without leaving the band for 100%.

  • Peel the bottle label off in one piece.

    Pull the label down along the perforation. Veer off and part of it stays on. Peel it all, then race the clock.

  • Find the end of the tape on the first try.

    Tap the moment the end of the spinning tape is under your fingernail. Your score is how far off you were, in mm.

  • Crack an egg with one hand. No shell allowed.

    Flick down and crack the egg on the rim of the bowl in one go. Too soft and you pry it open, too hard and the shell shatters.
