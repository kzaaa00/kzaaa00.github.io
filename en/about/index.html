About tapitta

What is this?

tapitta is a small site of browser games made for phones. Each game takes a few seconds to a minute and starts as soon as you open the link. Your result comes out as a number (a percentage, millimeters, seconds) plus a title, so you can compare with friends or share it on X.

All products, shops and people that appear in the games are made up.

Who makes it

kzaaa00, an indie developer in Japan. The original Japanese games are at tapitta.com.
